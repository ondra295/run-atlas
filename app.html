About Running Atlas

Running Atlas was born out of a simple frustration: modern sports apps are great at tracking weekly mileage, but terrible at preserving your personal running journey. In a fast-moving social feed, the marathon you trained four months for or the unforgettable ridge trail you ran at sunrise disappear within days.

We built this atlas as a digital, interactive canvas — a place where every finish line, high-altitude summit, and dream race has its permanent geographic coordinates. No likes, no algorithmic feeds, just your own visual map of mountains, asphalt, and memories.

„A runner’s life shouldn’t be buried in an activity stream. It deserves an atlas.“

Crafted independently for road maratoners, ultra-trail runners, and mountain wanderers. Version 1.6 • Leaflet & Supabase Cloud.

Support & Feedback

⌚ How to export .GPX from Garmin Connect? Open activity in Garmin Connect on web • Click gear icon in top right • Select "Export to GPX" • Drag & drop directly here.
📱 How to export from Strava? Open activity on strava.com • Click three dots (…) on the left • Select "Export GPX".
🍎 Apple Watch / Health app Use free tools like HealthFit or WorkOutDoors on iPhone to export any run directly to .GPX.
✉️ Direct email contact Need help with your data? Reach out directly to support@runningatlas.app.
