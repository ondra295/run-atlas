Unlike algorithmic social feeds that bury your greatest finish lines after 48 hours, Running Atlas preserves your running history on an interactive global map.

Geographic Memory

Every road marathon, trail summit, and vacation long run on a single topographic canvas. Zoom into city streets or survey your lifetime footprint across mountain ranges.

1-Click Race Catalog

No need to search for old GPX files. Select from curated World Marathon Majors, European alpine sky-races, and regional classics to light up your map in seconds.

Watch Tracks & Elevation

Import .GPX tracks from Garmin, Apple Watch, or Strava. Relive elevation profiles, official split targets vs. finish times, and memorable photos in clean dark mode.

How Running Atlas works

Three simple steps to curate your personal running autobiography.

STEP 01

Select or Upload

Pick races you've completed from our database, or drag and drop your watch's original GPX files for elevation curves and course routes.

STEP 02

Log Context & 'The Why'

Record what made the day memorable: official finish times, pacing satisfaction, photos, terrain notes, or future ambitions.

STEP 03

Map Your Future Bucket List

Pin races you dream of running. View travel logistics, estimated costs, and distance from your Home Base on the map.
