A runner’s life shouldn't be buried in a social feed.

Modern running apps are great at calculating your weekly training load, heart rate zones, and split paces. But they are surprisingly poor at preserving your personal running journey. In a fast-moving social stream, the marathon you spent sixteen weeks training for — or the high-altitude mountain pass you crossed at dawn — disappears behind yesterday's recovery jogs within days.

„We don't just run to collect kilometers. We run to explore landscapes, conquer summits, and mark our footsteps across the world.“

The Philosophy of Running Atlas

Running Atlas was created to give every runner a permanent digital canvas. An interactive atlas where every finish line, official race result, and dream bucket-list destination has its exact geographic coordinates.

🗺️ Geographic Memory Over Algorithm Feeds

No like counts, no influencer leaderboards, no sponsored posts. Just your own visual map of asphalt, trails, and contour lines connecting where you've been with where you are heading next.

🏔️ Watch Tracks & Topography

Directly upload watch GPS files (.GPX) from Garmin, Apple Watch, Coros, or Strava to display real elevation curves, finish times, and race memories in high definition.
