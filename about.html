A runner’s life shouldn't be buried in a social feed.

Modern running apps are great at calculating weekly training load and heart rate zones, but terrible at preserving your personal running journey. In a fast-moving social stream, the marathon you trained four months for disappears behind yesterday's recovery runs within days.

„We don't just run to collect kilometers. We run to explore landscapes, conquer summits, and mark our footsteps across the world.“

The Philosophy of Running Atlas

Running Atlas gives every runner a permanent digital canvas — an interactive world map where every finish line, official result, and dream bucket-list destination has its exact geographic coordinates.

Creator & Runner

Ondřej Hradecký

Dlouholetý běžec se zaměřením na maratony a horské traily. Running Atlas jsem vytvořil s cílem nabídnout běžecké komunitě nezávislý a čistý prostor bez algoritmů a reklam.
