Frequently Asked Questions & Guides

How do I import .GPX tracks from Garmin Connect?

Log in to your account at connect.garmin.com on a browser • Open the running activity • Click the small gear icon in the top right corner • Select "Export to GPX" • Simply drag and drop that file into the Atlas modal.

How do I export runs from Strava?

On strava.com, open your race activity • In the left sidebar, click the three dots (…) button • Click "Export GPX" to download your exact satellite track.

What is the Home Base used for?

Your Home Base centers the world map automatically upon opening and calculates straight-line or travel logistics to all upcoming and dream bucket-list events.

Is my athletic data private?

Yes. Your running activities, GPS paths, and notes belong strictly to you. No tracking algorithms, no ad targeting, and no sharing with third-party data brokers.
