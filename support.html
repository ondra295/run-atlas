Watch & GPX Export Guides

⌚ Garmin Connect (.GPX) Open your activity on connect.garmin.com in a web browser • Click the small gear icon in the top right corner • Select "Export to GPX" • Drag & drop directly into Running Atlas.
📱 Strava (.GPX) Open any running activity on strava.com on desktop • Click the three dots (…) button on the left sidebar • Click "Export GPX".
🍎 Apple Watch & Health Use free tools such as HealthFit, RunGap, or WorkOutDoors on your iPhone to export running tracks directly as .GPX files.
